---
title: "Plan work that does not fit one session"
---

`/grill-with-docs` is for a change you can settle in one session. [`/wayfinder`](/docs/explanations/engineering/wayfinder) is for an effort whose destination you can name, but whose path does not fit in one conversation: a greenfield build, a migration, a large feature.

```text
/wayfinder
```

It charts the work as a shared map of decision tickets on the issue tracker, then resolves them one at a time. A decision ticket holds a question. Resolving it is a decision, not a slice of implementation. Wayfinder can hand a part of the map back to `/grill-with-docs` when that part does fit in one session.

Reaching for wayfinder on a well-scoped feature is the common mistake. It is slower and denser than grilling. If you can settle the plan in this conversation, stay on [grill-with-docs](/docs/03-align).

When you are not sure which of the two you are in, describe the situation to [`/ask-matt`](/docs/explanations/engineering/ask-matt). It routes across the user-invoked skills instead of guessing.
